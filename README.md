One day's world news translated into a broken pipe organ — cracked, scorched and warped pipes, with a single straight silver pipe rising to the sky for the hope of spaceflight — and the six stories on flat ivory cards in large, legible type. Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 25 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

## The six stories

- **Hormuz:** Two oil tankers hit naval mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz (IRGC statement).
- **Gaza:** Israeli strike killed Hamas security chief Mohammad Abuelouf, his wife and two children. 4 killed.
- **Odesa:** Russian strike killed 2, injured 3 including a child.
- **Sudan:** RSF drone strikes killed 19 civilians, wounded 20.
- **Space:** Shenzhou-24 carried Pakistan's first astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to China's Tiangong station. First foreign astronaut aboard Tiangong.
- **Markets:** South Korea's KOSPI plunged 6.16% to 4,697.51. Samsung -9.87%, SK Hynix -11.05%.

Every number in the image was verified against reporting on the day; no unverified figure is used. Figures that sources disagree on are left out.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` — it copies `news.png` (1024×1024) to `/output/entry.png`. Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write elsewhere. The program is deterministic: no network, no randomness, no timestamps, so two runs write byte-identical files.
